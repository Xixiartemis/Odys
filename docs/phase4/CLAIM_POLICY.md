# Phase 4 Claim Policy

Before P4.3 publishes machine-readable results, all comparative performance
claims are `NOT_PROVEN`. This includes reliability improvement, cost
reduction, resume superiority, and benchmark advantage.

Claims are classified as:

- `PROVEN`: directly supported by published, valid machine-readable results
  and the frozen protocol identity.
- `SUPPORTED_BUT_NOT_HEADLINE`: supported architectural or operational
  observations that are not a headline comparative result.
- `NOT_PROVEN`: any unmeasured comparative or long-horizon claim.

Phase 3 architectural claims may be stated within their evidence scope:
typed workflow execution, trusted evidence, independent verification, failure
provenance, selective repair, and durable Attempt lineage. Delegation
lifecycle capability must not be described as a reliable multi-agent runtime.
No percentage, winner, or cost claim may be written until it is present in
published valid results.
